# Project Charter

## Project Title

AI-Powered Hospital Management System (AI-HMS)

## Project Overview

AI-HMS is a web-based Hospital Management System designed to streamline hospital operations through digital management of patients, doctors, appointments, medical records, prescriptions, and administrative activities. The system will also incorporate Artificial Intelligence capabilities to assist healthcare staff with information retrieval, medical record summarization, report generation, and intelligent scheduling.

## Problem Statement

Many hospitals and clinics still rely on fragmented software systems or manual processes for managing patient information, appointments, and clinical workflows. These approaches can lead to inefficiencies, data duplication, delayed decision-making, and increased administrative workload.

## Project Goal

To develop a modern, scalable, and secure Hospital Management System that centralizes hospital operations and leverages AI technologies to improve productivity, decision-making, and patient care.

## Objectives

### Primary Objectives

* Manage patient records digitally.
* Manage doctor profiles and schedules.
* Handle appointment booking and tracking.
* Maintain medical records and prescriptions.
* Provide role-based access for hospital staff.
* Generate operational reports.

### AI Objectives

* AI-powered hospital assistant.
* Medical record summarization.
* Intelligent appointment recommendations.
* AI-generated reports and insights.

## Scope

### In Scope

* User authentication and authorization.
* Patient management.
* Doctor management.
* Appointment management.
* Medical records management.
* Prescription management.
* Dashboard and reporting.
* AI assistant integration.

### Out of Scope (Version 1)

* Telemedicine/video consultation.
* Insurance claim processing.
* IoT medical device integration.

* System is deployable and usable in a real-world clinical environment.
